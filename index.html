What these numbers mean

Bankroll is paper money; every room started at $1,000. Shadow rooms grade bets at $0 until their live switch passes.

Closing-line value (CLV) asks whether the line moved your way after you bet; it is the best early sign of a real edge. "Not beating the close yet" = average CLV at or below zero.
