The rules

    Team ratings

    Every FBS team. Offense is points scored above an average FBS team; defense is points kept off the board compared with average (higher is better). Power = offense + defense, so it's roughly how many points better than average a team is on a neutral field. Change is the move since last week.

      How the simulation works

      Power rankings

      The model’s top 25 by power rating (points better than an average FBS team on a neutral field), next to the AP poll, the Coaches Poll and the CFP committee. Power measures how good a team is; the polls and committee mostly reward what a team has done, so big gaps are expected.

      Season check

      One row per weekly run: biggest rating risers and fallers, and the biggest gaps between the model, the committee and the market.

      Awards & futures

      Paper tracking only. Futures and award picks never touch the bankroll. Model views on awards are experimental.
